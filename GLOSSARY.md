# Skills collection

Language used when authoring, validating, and releasing the collection.

## Language

**Skill package**: The complete instructions and supporting resources delivered together for one named task.

**Collection acceptance**: Evidence that the collection's packages meet its authoring contract, preserve their resources during installation, and pass the applicable checks.

**Recipe regression**: A check of documented commands against controlled inputs. It does not evaluate an agent's judgment.

**Scripted replay**: A predetermined action plan applied to a disposable scenario and checked against independent expected outcomes.

**Model evaluation**: A recorded run in which a model receives a task and the skill, chooses its actions, and is assessed against expected outcomes.
_Avoid_: Using “model evaluation” for prompt-definition validation or scripted replay.

**Release evidence**: Results whose tested source, method, environment, and limitations identify which release claims they support.
